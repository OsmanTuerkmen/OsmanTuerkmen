## About me

I'm a Business Informatics student based in Vienna. I enjoy solving practical problems with data and turning good ideas into useful software.

## Selected projects

### [Signly](https://github.com/OsmanTuerkmen/signly) · Hackathon

A browser-based prototype that recognizes 12 isolated ASL signs. Hand and pose landmarks feed a local model, so camera frames stay on the device.

### [Flood Risk Project](https://github.com/OsmanTuerkmen/Floodrisk-project) · WU × infrared.city

Our Data Science Lab team explored urban flood susceptibility using satellite imagery, elevation, rainfall, and map data. I focused on geospatial analysis and feature engineering.

### [Predict the Unpredictable](https://github.com/OsmanTuerkmen/chess-outcome-prediction) · Chess analytics

I modeled in-game win probabilities using more than 130,000 Lichess games, engine evaluations, player strength, and time pressure.
